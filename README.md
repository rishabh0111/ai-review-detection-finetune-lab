# Real or AI? Fine-tuning models to catch AI-written fake reviews

AI-written fake product reviews are hard for people to spot. This project fine-tunes models to catch them,
including fakes from AI models they never saw in training, and compares three fine-tuning approaches:

| Method | Model |
|---|---|
| Full fine-tune of an encoder classifier | ModernBERT-large |
| Supervised fine-tuning with LoRA | Qwen3.5-4B |
| GRPO (reinforcement learning with a verifiable reward; the model explains its verdict) | Qwen3.5-4B |

They are measured against a simple bag-of-words baseline, untuned models and people, on three test sets:
in-distribution, fakes from other AI models (the headline), and short reviews.

**Status:** planning and setup. Progress, decisions and next steps are in [TRACKER.md](TRACKER.md).

## Data

[`Flowerly/modern-fake-reviews`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Flowerly/modern-fake-reviews) (CC-BY-4.0):
real Amazon-style reviews paired with length- and rating-matched fakes generated by DeepSeek-v4-pro. A copy at
revision `bf57dfb` is in [`data/raw/`](data/raw/), unchanged, with its dataset card.
